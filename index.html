ABOUT BOOKAROO

Made for Red Raiders. Built around better textbook exchanges.

Bookaroo connects Texas Tech students to buy and sell textbooks directly with one another. Find your course, check the edition, and coordinate with the student you're buying from.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the right textbook

Search by course, ISBN, or title and verify the required edition before purchasing.

02 / CONNECT

Ask the seller

Use Bookaroo messages to ask about condition, edition, and availability before you buy.

03 / MEET SAFELY

Campus meetup guidance

Agree on a public, well-lit campus meeting spot and a convenient time. Check the book's condition and edition together. Avoid sharing sensitive contact or payment information in messages.

Important: TTU email verification verifies access to the email address; Bookaroo is an independent student marketplace, not an official Texas Tech service. Payments should use the site's supported checkout when available.
